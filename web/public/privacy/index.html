ITS Maps

Privacy Policy / Kebijakan Privasi

This page explains how ITS Maps handles data for the web app, Windows desktop app, Microsoft Store package, Windows widgets, traffic dashboard, Raspberry Pi sync, and camera AI features.

Developer Hanifa Septhi Larasati
Publisher Hanifa Teams
Last updated 8 July 2026

1. Information We Process

Depending on which features are enabled, ITS Maps may process:

  • Device telemetry such as device name, device identifier, online status, battery or network status, and Raspberry Pi sync status.
  • Map and location data published by configured ITS Maps devices or entered by the user for map display and tracking features.
  • Traffic and detection summaries, including counts, labels, confidence values, timestamps, and dashboard history.
  • Camera stream URLs or local camera access selected by the user. Camera frames may be analyzed locally in the app for AI detection where supported.
  • Presentation collaboration data, including project title, slide content, comments, participant display name, and optional Google account identity when the presentation feature is used.
  • App configuration, diagnostics, version/update metadata, and widget state needed to operate the Microsoft Store app and Windows widgets.

2. How We Use Information

ITS Maps uses information to provide realtime maps, traffic charts, camera AI detection, Raspberry Pi monitoring, widgets, notifications, presentation collaboration, app updates, and troubleshooting.

3. Firebase and Third-Party Services

ITS Maps uses Firebase services, including Firebase Hosting and Firebase Realtime Database, to host the app and synchronize realtime data. Optional Google sign-in may be used for presentation collaboration. Microsoft Store services may process package, licensing, crash, and install information according to Microsoft policies.

4. Camera, AI, and Location

Camera and location features are used only for app functionality selected or configured by the user. ITS Maps does not sell camera, AI, location, or device telemetry data. Raw camera video is not intentionally stored by ITS Maps unless the user connects the app to a service or device configuration that stores it separately.

5. Sharing

We may share data only with service providers needed to run the app, when required by law, to protect users and systems, or with the user's configured devices and collaborators. We do not sell personal information.

6. Retention and Deletion

Realtime and history data is retained only as long as needed for the configured ITS Maps features, Firebase records, diagnostics, or user projects. Users can remove locally stored app data by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app. Data synchronized to Firebase can be removed by deleting the related configured records or by contacting the publisher through the Microsoft Store listing.

7. Security

ITS Maps uses HTTPS and platform security features where available. No online service can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure, so users should only connect trusted devices, camera streams, and Firebase projects.

8. Children's Privacy

ITS Maps is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact the publisher through the Microsoft Store listing.

9. Contact

For privacy requests, support, deletion requests, or questions about this policy, contact Hanifa Teams through the ITS Maps Microsoft Store listing or publisher support channel.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated when ITS Maps changes features, services, or legal requirements. The updated policy will be posted at this URL.
